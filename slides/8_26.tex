\section{Predicting where the policy fails}
% ##################################################################

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Idea: score every scene's failure risk \emph{before} training}
  \small
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Labels only cover \textbf{154 of 1000} shards (73{,}920 closed-loop
          diffusion evals). We cannot reweight 2.3M training examples from
          3{,}135 failure labels directly.
    \item So fit a cheap risk model on \textbf{a-priori descriptors of the
          logged expert trajectory} --- computable for \emph{every} scene,
          including the 846 shards never evaluated.
  \end{itemize}
  \begin{block}{Four descriptor families (one per failure hypothesis)}
    \begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
      \toprule
      family & measures & targets \\
      \midrule
      B clearance & bbox-corner signed dist.\ to road edge & the 89\% that reach goal then violate \\
      C maneuver  & curvature, heading change, braking     & turns: where lateral error is largest \\
      D ambiguity & lane-polyline count, heading spread    & valid-but-wrong branch at a fork \\
      E exposure  & goal distance, path length, tortuosity & the 10.6\% that never reach goal \\
      \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
  \end{block}
  Descriptors reproduce waymax's \emph{own} offroad test (signed corner-to-edge
  distance), so they are the real margin, not a proxy.
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{The risk model and its accuracy}
  \small
  \begin{columns}[T]
    \column{0.50\textwidth}
    L2 logistic regression, fit on the \textbf{expert-clean} slice only
    ($n=9{,}898$, 325 failures, \textbf{3.28\%} base rate). Shard-grouped
    5-fold CV:
    \begin{center}
    \begin{tabular}{@{}lrr@{}}
      \toprule
      family & AUC & top-decile lift \\
      \midrule
      \textbf{ALL} & \textbf{0.860} & \textbf{5.05$\times$} \\
      C maneuver   & 0.828 & 4.00$\times$ \\
      B clearance  & 0.757 & 3.02$\times$ \\
      E exposure   & 0.730 & 2.53$\times$ \\
      D ambiguity  & 0.601 & 1.26$\times$ \\
      \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \end{center}
    \textbf{The top risk decile holds $\sim$50\% of all failures} --- 10\% of
    the data, half the failures.

    \column{0.50\textwidth}
    Largest coefficients:
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
      \item[$+$] heading\_change\_total (1.26)
      \item[$-$] heading\_change\_net ($-0.83$)
      \item[$+$] curvature\_max (0.83)
      \item[$-$] stationary\_frac ($-0.60$)
      \item[$+$] road\_frames\_below\_1m (0.46)
    \end{itemize}
    \vspace{0.4em}
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
      \item Scores \textbf{482{,}153} scenes $\rightarrow$ \texttt{risk.npz}.
      \item Dirty gate: expert bbox already offroad
            (\texttt{road\_clearance\_min}$<0$) $\Rightarrow$ excluded, 1.5\%.
      \item Agent-proximity features useless (AUC $\approx$0.55) despite
            collisions being 29\% of failures.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Turning risk into a sampling weight}
  \small
  One knob $\alpha$ on the per-scene risk:
  \[
    w(x) \;=\; \mathrm{clip}\!\left(\left(\tfrac{\mathrm{risk}(x)}{\overline{\mathrm{risk}}}\right)^{\!\alpha},\;
    \tfrac{1}{c},\; c\right), \qquad c=10 .
  \]
  \begin{itemize}
    \item $\alpha=0$: a plain permutation --- \textbf{exactly} the unweighted
          run (a true control, not an approximation).
    \item $\alpha=1$: sample in proportion to predicted failure risk.
    \item Dirty scenes get weight \textbf{0} (excluded, never upweighted).
    \item Resampling, not loss weighting: shards are preprocessed in memory, so
          drawing indices by weight is free.
  \end{itemize}
  \begin{alertblock}{Caveat carried from the ranking}
    AUC 0.860 says the model finds \emph{where} the policy fails. It does
    \textbf{not} say more behavior-cloning weight there \emph{fixes} it --- these
    are compounding closed-loop errors. That is what the sweep had to measure.
  \end{alertblock}
\end{frame}

\section{The shared simulator reward}
% ##################################################################

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{How the reward is computed}
  \small
  One batched, JIT-able \texttt{reward\_step} that every fine-tuning method
  consumes (single-scene $\equiv$ batched, provably). Per step, the return is the
  sum of additive components:
  \[
    r_t \;=\; \underbrace{r^{\text{prog}}_t}_{\text{progress}}
    \;-\; \underbrace{\lambda\,\|a_t\|^2}_{\text{action}}
    \;+\; \underbrace{r^{\text{coll}}_t}_{\le 0}
    \;+\; \underbrace{r^{\text{off}}_t}_{\le 0}
    \;+\; \underbrace{r^{\text{goal}}_t}_{\text{bonus}}
  \]
  \begin{columns}[T]
    \column{0.55\textwidth}
    \textbf{Progress} --- two modes:
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep1pt
      \item goal mode: $\;p\,(d_{t-1}-d_t)$ \; (goal-distance closed)
      \item route mode: $\;p\,(s_t-s_{t-1}) - \text{lateral pen.}$\\
            (on-route arclength; a beeline offroad earns none)
    \end{itemize}
    \textbf{One-time events} (charged once/episode by default):
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep1pt
      \item collision, offroad --- or \emph{per-step} to charge dwell time.
    \end{itemize}
    A terminated world contributes 0 thereafter, so $\sum_t r_t$ = return.

    \column{0.45\textwidth}
    \begin{block}{Default weights}
    \begin{tabular}{@{}lr@{}}
      progress $p$      & $1.0$ \\
      action penalty    & $0.01$ \\
      collision         & $-10.0$ \\
      offroad           & $-5.0$ \\
      goal bonus        & $+10.0$ \\
      goal threshold    & $3.0$ m \\
      \midrule
      terminate on coll.& yes \\
      terminate on offrd& no \\
    \end{tabular}
    \end{block}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Why the reward shape matters (V-Max parity)}
  \small
  The default goal-distance reward is \textbf{farmable}: unbounded progress plus
  an offroad charge paid \emph{once} and non-terminating makes cutting the corner
  across a median strictly pay.
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}lrrr@{}}
    \toprule
    run & progress & offroad-step frac & solved \\
    \midrule
    default reward (\texttt{dppo500}) & $+15$ & 66\% & \textbf{0} \\
    V-Max SAC parity (\texttt{lq})    & ---   & 0.85\% & \textbf{94.3\%} \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}
  \textbf{Fix (parity recipe):} route-arclength progress (offroad beeline earns
  nothing), offroad \emph{terminates} (real cost = forfeited progress, $\sim$16$\times$
  the flat penalty), smaller magnitudes, plus potential-based goal shaping that
  telescopes against per-replan GAE. Wired behind flags that default to the exact
  old behavior.
\end{frame}
